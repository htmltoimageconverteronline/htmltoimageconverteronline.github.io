Frequently asked questions

Is this HTML to image converter free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Is my HTML uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs in your browser. Only the Tailwind CSS script and your Google Fonts are loaded from their own servers.

Does it support Tailwind CSS?

Yes. Use Tailwind classes in your HTML and they are applied in the preview and in the downloaded image.

How do I get a transparent background?

Tick Transparent background, then download PNG, WEBP or SVG. JPG cannot be transparent, so it uses the background colour.

Why does an image from another website not appear?

Browsers block images from sites that do not allow outside use. Host the image yourself or embed it as a data URL.

What is the difference between Content only and Full page?

Content only crops the image to your main card or block. Full page also includes the page background and the space around it.
